A second pass, built from your references

Four directions for the console,
each quietly expensive.

The first attempt was dark, neon and techy — which is the opposite of nearly every reference you sent. Off-white grounds, pure white cards, one deep brand colour, a classic serif against a clean grotesque, and a great deal of space. Same data, same program, four different personalities.

Paper

Warm cream ground, navy ink, an editorial serif for the greeting, hairline rules instead of borders. Reads like a well-set annual report.

Warmest · best for trust

Studio

Pure white, one deep navy stage carrying the single most important action, and a yellow used exactly once. Precise, symmetrical, unhurried.

Cleanest · best for focus

Atelier

Soft sage, blush, sky and butter cards — each track its own colour — with families as rounded cards you can read at a glance.

Friendliest · best for families

Nocturne

The one dark direction — deep navy, an elegant serif, fine gold hairlines and gold used once. Cinematic rather than technical.

Most premium · best for donors
What changed and why
Ground — off-white paper and pure white instead of near-black. Every reference leans light.
Type — an editorial serif for display against a clean grotesque for the interface, with mono reserved for codes and labels.
Colour — one deep brand colour doing all structural work; the yellow appears once per screen, never as a theme.
Space — bigger padding, fewer panels, one primary action per view. The old versions tried to show everything at once.
