Beyond Limits · design lab

Four ways to upgrade
the console.

Same data, same navy-and-solar identity, four completely different organising ideas. Every one uses live liquid glass over an animated wallpaper, cuts the navigation down, and gives each screen exactly one primary action.

VARIATION 01

Command Deck

Icon dock + bento grid. Everything at a glance for someone who already knows the product. Dense but calm: 6 dock icons, 6 tiles, one hero action.

Best for · daily overview
VARIATION 02

Today

Radically reduced: three tabs, one page, a numbered list of what needs a human. Turns a 24-screen console into a morning briefing.

Best for · "just tell me what to do"
VARIATION 03

Spotlight

A live widget field under a ⌘K command palette. No menus to learn — type "issue codes" or "Ethan" and go straight there.

Best for · keyboard speed
VARIATION 04

Atlas

Four plain-English groups instead of 24 links, each saying what it is for, plus a lean mode that hides anything unused for 30 days.

Best for · onboarding new staff
